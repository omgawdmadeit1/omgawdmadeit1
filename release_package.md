### Stage 2: BEAT
- Drum blueprint:
  - Kick: 1, “&” of 2, 3a variations before hook turnaround.
  - Snare/Clap: beats 3 (trap halftime feel).
  - Hats: 1/8 base + 1/16 bursts every 2 bars; occasional 1/32 roll into transitions.
  - Perc: rim ghost on “&” of 4 in verse bars 4/8/12/16.
- 808 behavior:
  - Long-note sustain in hook root notes.
  - Glide approach tones on bar-end pickups (E→F#, C#→D).
  - Brief octave jump on last hook bar for lift.
- Chord progression (8-bar loop):
  - **F#m – D – A – E** (i–VI–III–VII)
  - MIDI guide (root position):
    - F#m: F#2 A2 C#3
    - D: D2 F#2 A2
    - A: A2 C#3 E3
    - E: E2 G#2 B2
- Sound design cues:
  - Main pluck: short decay, slight detune, ping-pong delay 1/8.
  - Pad: low-passed stereo pad opening 2 kHz→6 kHz through pre-hook.
  - FX: reverse vocal swell before every hook; filtered drop at bridge start.
- Transition map:
  - Verse→Pre: remove kick 1 bar, keep hats + riser.
  - Pre→Hook: snare fill + sub drop.
  - Bridge→Final Hook: half-time chop then full drums slam.

### Stage 4: MIX
- Gain staging:
  - Keep lead vocal peak around -10 to -8 dBFS pre-bus.
  - Drum bus peaks around -6 dBFS before mix bus processing.
- EQ zones:
  - Lead vocal HPF 80 Hz; tame 250–350 Hz mud; presence boost 3–5 kHz.
  - 808 carve around vocal fundamental pocket (typically 120–220 Hz dynamic ducking).
  - Hats tame harshness around 8–10 kHz if brittle.
- Compression targets:
  - Lead vocal: 3–6 dB GR (1176-style fast attack/release into LA-2A-style leveling).
  - Drum bus: 1–3 dB GR for glue.
- FX sends:
  - Short plate (0.9s) for body.
  - 1/8 delay throw on hook tail words (“sunrise,” “gold,” “mine”).
- Stereo plan:
  - Mono center: kick, snare core, lead, 808.
  - Wide: pads, ad-libs, delays, hook doubles.
- Loudness targets:
  - Streaming master: -9 to -8 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ -1.0 dBTP.
  - Short-form clip master: -8 to -7 LUFS, true peak ≤ -1.0 dBTP.
